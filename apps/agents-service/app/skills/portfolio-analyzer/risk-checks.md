# Risk Check Reference

## Concentration Thresholds

| Level            | Threshold | Action                     |
| ---------------- | --------- | -------------------------- |
| Single stock     | > 15%     | Flag as high concentration |
| Single sector    | > 30%     | Flag as sector overweight  |
| Single geography | > 70%     | Flag as home-country bias  |

## Beta Interpretation

- Beta < 0.8: Defensive portfolio
- Beta 0.8–1.2: Market-neutral
- Beta > 1.2: Aggressive / high volatility

## Common Risk Factors to Mention

- Interest rate sensitivity (for REITs, utilities, bonds)
- Commodity exposure (energy, materials)
- Currency risk (international holdings)
- Liquidity risk (small caps, micro caps)
